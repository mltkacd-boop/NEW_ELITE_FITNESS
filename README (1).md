# ⚔️ ELITE FITNESS
### Train like Toji. Level up like Jin-Woo.

**Elite Fitness** is a dark, gamified fitness web app that makes working out feel like an RPG. Every session earns you XP, levels up your character, and brings you closer to peak physical form — inspired by the most iconic warriors in anime.

---

## 🚀 How to Use the App

### 1. Sign Up or Skip
When you open the app, you'll see a login screen.
- **Create an account** (email + password) to save your progress across devices via cloud sync.
- **Skip** to use the app locally — your data is saved in your browser (no account needed).

---

## 🏠 Home Page

Your command center. Here you'll see:

- **Your Player Card** — Your name, level, XP bar, and 3 key stats (total workouts, current streak, days trained).
- **Next Workout Banner** — Shows today's recommended workout. Tap it to jump straight in.
- **Streak Calendar** — A heatmap showing every day you've trained. Gold squares = completed. Keep the chain alive!
- **Daily Challenge** — A bonus challenge from your active character (Toji or Jin-Woo). Complete it for extra XP.
- **Mode Banner** — Shows which character you've activated and your current training split.

---

## 🏋️ Workouts Page

Browse and start your training sessions.

- **Your Weekly Plan** — A day-by-day strip showing what to train each day of the week based on your chosen program.
- **Today's Workout** is highlighted so you always know what's next.
- **Workout Cards** show the workout name, muscle groups targeted, number of exercises, and estimated XP reward.
- Tap **START WORKOUT** to begin a live session.

### During a Session
- Each exercise is shown one by one with the number of sets and reps.
- Log your weight and reps for each set.
- A **rest timer** starts automatically between sets.
- Form tips appear below each exercise.
- When you're done, tap **FINISH SESSION** to collect your XP reward.

## ⚔️ Character Modes

Choose your fighter — this changes the entire look and feel of the app, your training split, and the motivational experience.

### 🔴 Toji Mode (Toji Fushiguro)
- Deep crimson red theme
- Raw, strength-focused training split
- Daily "Toji Challenges" to push your limits
- **"Become Toji" meter** — tracks how close your stats are to Toji's physical level
- **Battle Scars** — special badges you unlock by hitting milestones
- **Unlockable Quotes** — earn motivational quotes from Toji as you level up

### 🟣 Jin-Woo Mode (Sung Jin-Woo)
- Dark shadow purple theme
- Systematic leveling split (like the Daily Quests from Solo Leveling)
- Shadow Monarch rank progression
- Weekly missions and power tracking

### 🏆 Default Mode (Gold)
- Classic elite gold theme
- Standard training split — great for beginners or if you just want to train without a character

**To switch modes:** Go to the Profile tab → tap "Change Mode" → pick your character and training goal.

## 📊 Progress & Stats

Track everything from the **Progress** tab:

- **XP & Level** — See your total XP, current level, and how much you need to reach the next one.
- **Rank Tiers** — Climb from Recruit → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Elite as you train more.
- **Workout History** — A log of every session you've completed with date, workout name, and XP earned.
- **Streak Stats** — Your current streak, longest streak ever, and total days trained.

## 📱 Install as an App (Mobile & Desktop)

Elite Fitness is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — meaning you can install it on your phone or computer just like a real app, with no app store required.

**On iPhone/iPad:**
1. Open the app in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**

**On Android:**
1. Open in Chrome
2. Tap the 3-dot menu
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**

**On Desktop (Chrome/Edge):**
1. Look for the install icon in the address bar
2. Click **Install**

Once installed, the app works **offline** — your data is always accessible even without internet.

## ❓ FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
No. You can use the app fully without signing up — data is saved locally in your browser. Create an account only if you want to sync across multiple devices.

**Will I lose my data if I clear my browser?**
If you're using local mode (no account), yes. Sign up and your data syncs to the cloud automatically.

**Can I use this on my phone?**
Yes — it's fully mobile-optimised with a bottom navigation bar. Install it to your home screen for the best experience.

**Is it free?**
Yes, completely free to use.

**Can I change my training mode later?**
Yes, anytime from your Profile tab. Your XP and progress carry over.
