# 🏋️ ELITE FITNESS

**Train. Level Up. Dominate.**

Elite Fitness is a free gamified fitness web app that turns your workouts into an RPG. Every session earns XP, every milestone ranks you up, and a built-in coach analyses your real training data to tell you exactly what to do next — no subscriptions, no ads, no AI API required.

Open it in any browser. Works on phone, tablet, and desktop. Works offline.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Open the app** — just open `index.html` in your browser (or visit the hosted link)
2. **Sign up** — create a free account with email or Google
3. **Complete onboarding** — pick your training goal, enter your details
4. **Start training** — go to Workouts, tap today's session, tap START
5. **Log sets as you finish them** — tap each set tile when done
6. **Finish** — earn XP, see your progress, get coaching feedback

That's it. The app handles everything else automatically.

## 📱 The Tabs

### 🏠 Home
Your dashboard. Shows:
- Your level, XP bar, and rank
- Today's scheduled workout
- Training streak calendar
- This week's completion and XP chart
- Recent session history
- Quick actions

### 💪 Workouts
Your full training plan for the week. Tap any day to expand, see all exercises with video links, then tap **START** to begin a live session.

**Inside a session:**
- Weight input per exercise (auto-suggested from your history)
- Tap set tiles as you complete them
- Rest timer fires automatically between sets
- Can't do an exercise? Tap **⇄ Can't do this?** to swap it
- Add a session note before finishing
- Mini chart shows your last 5 sessions per exercise

### ⭐ Level Up
Your progress stats:
- XP rank (Bronze → Mythic)
- Muscle XP bars for every muscle group
- Exercise progress chart — select any exercise to see your strength over time
- Weight tracking chart
- Personal records

### 🧠 Elite Coach
Your personal data-driven coach. No AI — 100% based on your actual training numbers.

Analyses and tells you:
- **Today's Focus** — what to train based on your programme and the day
- **Warnings** — overtraining risk, long inactivity, losing streaks
- **Last Session Rating** — Excellent / Good / Incomplete + specific recommendation
- **Strength Progression** — which exercises are improving, plateauing, or regressing
- **Recovery Status** — whether you need rest or can push harder
- **PR Analysis** — what to target next
- **Arm Wrestling Coach** — loss patterns, weakest stats, what to train
- **Recommended Actions** — top 5 things to do right now, with direct links

### 🥗 Nutrition
Daily food and macro tracking:
- Calorie and macro targets auto-calculated from your stats and goal
- Log meals by category (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks)
- Quick-add database of common foods
- Save your own **meal presets** — log your usual breakfast in one tap
- Water intake tracker
- 7-day nutrition chart

### 📋 History
Every session you've ever completed. Tap any entry to expand and see:
- Sets, duration, XP earned
- Volume logged (weight × reps per exercise)
- Your session note
- Which muscles were trained

### 👤 Profile
Your character sheet:
- RPG stats (STR, END, AGI, VIT) derived from real training data
- Animated character that evolves as you level up
- Body stats (height, weight, BMI)
- Programme progress (which phase you're in)
- Personal records
- Arm Wrestling profile

## 📈 The Progression System

The app uses a **12-week periodized programme** that automatically adjusts every 4 weeks:

| Phase | Weeks | What Changes |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Foundation | 1–4 | Learn movements, moderate weight |
| Volume | 5–8 | More sets and reps, build capacity |
| Intensity | 9–12 | Heavier weight, fewer reps |
| ⚡ Deload | 4, 8, 12 | 60% weight, full recovery |

**Weight progression:**
- Hit all target reps → app suggests +weight next session
- Missed reps → holds current weight
- Stalled 3+ sessions → plateau warning with specific fix advice

After 12 weeks, the cycle resets and starts again.

## ❓ FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
No. Tap "Skip" on the login screen to use the app in local mode. Create an account only if you want cloud sync across devices.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Everything except the initial sign-in works offline. Your data saves locally and syncs when you're back online.

**Can I change my goal later?**
Yes. Go to Workouts → tap "CHANGE MODE" to switch your training plan at any time.

**What if I miss a day?**
Just pick up where you left off. The app detects inactivity and the Elite Coach will tell you to restart at 80% of your last weight — not to punish yourself by doubling volume.

**How does the weight suggestion work?**
The app looks at your last session. If you hit all target reps, it suggests adding weight. If you missed reps, it holds the same weight. Simple and automatic.

**What is a deload week?**
Every 4th week, weights automatically drop to 60%. This is intentional — it lets your joints, tendons, and nervous system recover so you come back stronger. The app explains this the first time it happens.
